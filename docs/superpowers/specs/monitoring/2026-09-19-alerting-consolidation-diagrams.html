Diagrams for the 2026-09-19 design spec. Four pictures: what exists today, the end state, how delivery is decided, and how legacy rules become monitors.

In an RMM, a monitor exists to produce an alert. So Alerts is the domain, with three facets: Monitors (what we watch for and what happens), the Inbox (what is wrong now), and Delivery (who gets told). Config policies answer one question: which devices. "Monitoring" means seeing data, and lives where the data is.
